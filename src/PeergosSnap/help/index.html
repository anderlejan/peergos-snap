Quick start

Peergos Snap lives in the Windows notification area (the icons next to the clock). It takes pictures and short videos of any part of your screen and gives you a link to share them, or puts the picture or video itself on the clipboard.

  1. Sign in to Peergos. Right-click the Peergos Snap icon → Settings… → Peergos. Enter your username and password and click Sign in. You do this once.
  2. Take a picture. Press Ctrl+Shift+1 (or left-click the icon). Drag a rectangle over the part of the screen you want and let go of the mouse.
  3. Share it. A card at the bottom right says Link copied to the clipboard. Paste the link with Ctrl+V into a chat, an e-mail or a document.
  4. Record a video. Press Ctrl+Shift+2, drag over the area to record and let go: recording starts. Press Ctrl+Shift+2 again (or click Stop), then choose Upload & copy link.
  5. Want the picture itself instead of a link? Right-click the icon → Output → Media to clipboard. Now every capture is copied as a picture or video file, ready to paste.

Talking with a friend who also uses Peergos Snap? Send pictures straight to their screen: see Direct to a friend.

Want to capture a menu that closes when you click elsewhere? Choose a delay first: right-click the icon → Delay → e.g. 3 seconds (see Capturing a menu). All your captures and their links are in History… (see History). Press F1 in the Settings window for help on the page you are on.

The Peergos Snap icon

The icon is a small green camera in the notification area. If you don't see it, click the ^ arrow next to the clock and drag the camera onto the taskbar so it always stays visible.

Left click

A left click takes a picture, or starts a video, depending on what you chose under Tray click takes in the menu. While a video is recording, a left click stops it.

Taking a picture

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+1, left-click the icon (when it takes pictures), or choose Take picture in the menu.
  2. A short hint appears at the top of the screen. The screen does not freeze: games, 3D worlds and videos keep moving under the mouse, so you can wait for the right moment.
  3. Drag a rectangle over what you want. The size is shown next to it.
  4. Let go of the mouse. The picture is taken at that very moment.
  • A whole window: just click once without dragging. The window under the mouse is outlined before you click.
  • Cancel: press Esc or click the right mouse button.
  • Several monitors: you can drag across monitors.
  • Need a moment after letting go? Set a delay in Settings → Overlay, for example to let an animation reach the right frame.
  • Games: for the best results run the game in a window or borderless window rather than exclusive full screen.

Capturing a menu (delay)

Menus and pop-ups close as soon as you click somewhere else, so they can't be selected in the normal way. Use a delay:

  1. Right-click the icon → Delay → choose 1 to 5 seconds. (Any length up to 60 seconds: Settings → Capture.)
  2. Start a picture as usual (hotkey, icon or menu). A countdown appears at the bottom right, and the icon shows the seconds left.
  3. Now open the menu you want to capture.
  4. When the countdown ends, the screen freezes: it stays exactly as it was, with your menu. Select the area, or click the menu to take just the menu.

The delay stays chosen until you change it; the icon's tooltip reminds you. To cancel a countdown, click Cancel on the countdown card, click the icon, or press the picture hotkey again.

What happens next depends on the output: see Where your capture goes.

Changed your mind? Discard

The card that appears after a picture has a Discard button. It removes the picture everywhere it went: from this PC (also from your mirror folder), from Peergos (the link stops working), from the clipboard if it is still there, and from the history. By default nothing is kept, not even in the Recycle Bin; Settings → Files & history can send discarded captures to the Recycle Bin instead.

To decide before anything happens, turn on After a picture, ask what to do (Settings → Capture): you then see the picture and choose Upload & copy link, Copy picture, Save as… or Discard, as after a recording. Delete discards, Esc keeps the picture on this PC only.

Recording a video

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+2, choose Record video in the menu, or left-click the icon when it is set to take videos.
  2. Drag over the area to record and let go. Recording starts at once (or after the delay you set).
  3. A dashed frame marks the area, and a small bar next to it shows the time with Stop, Pause and Cancel. The frame and the bar never appear in the video, even when you record the whole screen.
  4. To finish, click Stop, press Ctrl+Shift+2 again, or left-click the icon.

After a recording

A short window asks what to do with the video:

Recording with a delay

If a delay is chosen (menu → Delay), you first select the area as usual; it stays marked while the countdown runs, and recording starts when it ends. Use the time to open a menu or get the scene ready.

Pause and cancel

Pause stops recording until you click Resume; the parts are joined into one video. You can also pause from the menu or with your own hotkey (Settings → Hotkeys). Cancel throws the recording away.

Mouse pointer

Choose whether the mouse pointer appears in videos: menu → Videos → Show mouse pointer, or Settings → Capture. Pictures never include the pointer.

Format and quality

Videos are MP4 by default (plays everywhere); WebM is available too. Frame rate and quality are in Settings → Capture.

Sound

Videos get the sound your PC plays while you record: a video you watch, a game, a call, a notification sound. Your microphone is not recorded. If nothing played during the whole recording, the video has no sound track at all. Turn it off in the menu (Videos → Record sound) or in Settings → Capture.

Where your capture goes

There are two outputs. Switch between them in the menu (Output), in Settings → Output, or with your own hotkey.

Secret link (upload to Peergos)

The capture is uploaded to your Peergos account, into the folder set in Settings → Peergos (normally PeergosSnap). A secret link to just that file is created and copied. It looks like this:

https://peergos.net/secret/z59…/1420289907#egfWi1Wsdjn?open=true

Anyone who has the link can open and view that one file in a web browser, without an account. They cannot change it and cannot see your other files.

Media to clipboard (no upload)

Pictures are copied as a picture: paste them into chat apps, image editors, documents or e-mails. Videos are copied as a file: paste them into a chat, an e-mail or a folder. Nothing is uploaded.

When an upload fails

If you are offline or Peergos cannot be reached, the picture or video is put on the clipboard instead, so you can still paste it (you can switch this off in Settings → Output). The card tells you why the upload failed.

Copies on your PC

  • Every capture is also kept in the captures folder: menu → Open captures folder. Captures are sorted into one folder per month, named like 2026-10; you can choose one folder per day or year, or none (Settings → Files & history).
  • Copies are kept forever unless you set a number of days in Settings → Files & history; older copies then go to the Recycle Bin, so nothing is lost by accident.
  • To keep every capture in a folder of your own as well, turn on Also save every capture to this folder (Settings → Files & history). It uses the same monthly folders.
  • Only keep a copy when the upload fails: Settings → Files & history → Keep a copy on this PC → Only if the upload fails. A capture that reached Peergos is then not kept in the captures folder; the history still has its link. Captures that are only copied to the clipboard or kept on this PC stay.
  • After a picture, ask what to do (Settings → Capture) lets you decide for each picture.

Your Peergos account

Links are created by your Peergos account, so Peergos Snap needs you to sign in once.

  1. Right-click the icon → Settings… → Peergos.
  2. Leave the server as it is (https://peergos.net) unless your account is on another Peergos server.
  3. Enter your username and password. Click the eye button next to the password to see what you typed.
  4. Click Sign in. If your account uses two-factor login, enter the code from your authenticator app when asked.
  • Your password is not kept. It is only used to sign in. Peergos Snap then stays signed in, protected for your Windows account, until you click Sign out.
  • If you change your password in Peergos, you are asked to sign in again.
  • Test connection checks that uploading works.
  • Folder for captures is where uploads go in your Peergos home. It is created when missing.
  • Accounts that only allow a security key (not an authenticator app) as second factor cannot sign in here; add an authenticator app to your Peergos account.

Your shared files in Peergos

Everything you upload is in the capture folder of your Peergos account, where you can open, rename or delete it in Peergos as usual. To stop sharing a file, delete it in Peergos.

History

Right-click the icon → History… lists every capture: a preview, when it was taken, the app it came from, its label and link, and where it is: on This PC, in Peergos, or both (the coloured tags).

  • Find a capture: type in the search box (label, file name, app or date), choose what to show (pictures, videos, only on this PC, only in Peergos, …) and how to sort (newest, oldest, name, size, app). With newest or oldest first, captures are grouped by day.
  • Details: click a capture to see a larger preview and everything about it. Double-click opens the file (or the link if the file is only in Peergos).
  • Old links: Copy link or Open link gives you the link again. Links of files uploaded before the history existed are found in your Peergos folder when the history opens.
  • Labels: type your own name or note into Label; it is saved as you type and shown in the list.
  • Upload later: a capture that is only on this PC can be uploaded with Upload & copy link.
  • Delete: From both deletes the capture on this PC (Recycle Bin) and in Peergos (its link stops working – this can't be undone) and removes the entry from the history; From this PC and From Peergos delete on one side only; Remove from history only removes the entry. Every delete asks first. Select several captures (Ctrl or Shift + click) to delete them together.
  • Delete key: Delete does the default action, which is highlighted: From both, unless you choose another one in Settings → Files & history. There you can also keep entries in the history after deleting from both places.
  • Tidy up: Clean up → Remove records whose files are gone removes entries that lead to no file any more; Clear the whole history empties the list. Neither deletes files, and removed entries are not added back.
  • Refresh looks at the captures folder and your Peergos folder again. The bottom line counts your captures and says when Peergos was last checked.

Don't want the app names remembered? Settings → Files & history → untick Remember which app each capture came from.

Direct to a friend

While you talk with someone in another app, you can show them a picture (or send a video or any other file) at once: send it, and within seconds it appears on their screen in the direct window. They don't have to do anything. Both of you can then zoom, save, label, pin and star it, and each of you can delete it (see below).

  1. Become friends once. Menu → Direct to a friend → Open the direct window → Friends…. Enter your friend's Peergos username and click Add. They accept your friend request (Peergos Snap shows it to them with an Accept button; it also works in Peergos itself). If you are friends in Peergos already, nothing needs to be accepted.
  2. Send a picture or file. Drop pictures, videos or other files on the dashed area of the direct window, paste one with Ctrl+V, click Choose files…, or click Take picture to select an area of the screen. From the menu: Direct to a friend → Send a picture to ….
  3. Your friend sees it. Their direct window comes up with your picture (without taking the keyboard from what they are typing) and a card says what arrived.

The direct window

  • Choose the friend at the top, and the month: what you send is kept in one folder per month.
  • Zoom: + and −, Ctrl + mouse wheel, Fit (F) or 100 % (0). Drag the picture to move around in it.
  • Label a picture by typing under it; Star it; Pin it so it stays at the top. Your friend sees your labels, stars and pins, and you see theirs.
  • Save as… keeps a copy wherever you like, Copy puts it on the clipboard, Open opens it in its app.
  • Delete (or Delete), after a question: something you sent is deleted from your Peergos, so it is gone for both of you. Something your friend sent is deleted on your side only (from this PC, and it is no longer shown to you on any PC); their original stays in their Peergos, and only they can delete it.

Who can see it

What you send stays in your Peergos, in a folder for that friend (PeergosSnap-Direct → the friend's name → one folder per month). The folder itself is not shared: each file you send is shared, read-only, with that one friend only, so they see exactly what you sent them and nothing else. No link is created. Pictures you open or receive are also kept on this PC, in the Direct folder next to the captures folder.

Both of you need Peergos Snap running and signed in to Peergos. To receive pictures at once, Peergos Snap keeps a connection to Peergos open while you have friends to share with; you can turn this off in Settings → Direct (pictures then arrive while the direct window is open).

Appearance

Choose a colour scheme and the font size for the whole app: Settings, this Help, User notes, the menu, the recording bar, dialogs and notification cards.

  • Dark: Peergos dark, Graphite, Midnight, Forest night, Plum night, High contrast.
  • Medium brightness: Slate, Storm blue, Moss, Mocha, Dusk violet, Teal stone.
  • Bright, soft on the eyes: Peergos light, Paper, Sepia, Mint, Sky, Lavender mist.
  • Follow Windows uses Peergos light or Peergos dark, matching your Windows setting.

Font size goes from 80 % to 160 %; 100 % resets it.

Updates

Peergos Snap looks for a new version when it starts and once a day. When it finds one, it downloads it, checks that the download is complete and genuine, installs it and starts again, usually within a minute. It never does this during a recording or an upload; it waits until you are done.

  • To decide yourself, turn off Install new versions automatically (Settings → General). A card then tells you about a new version, with an Install now button.
  • To look right now, click Check for the new version in Settings → General.
  • After an update a card tells you the new version number. Your settings, sign-in and notes are kept.

User notes

User notes is a list of changes you would like in Peergos Snap: things to fix, adjust or add, and questions. Open it from the menu (User notes…).

  • Add a note with + Add note. Give it a short title, details, a type (fix, adjustment, new feature, question), an area and a priority.
  • Tick notes to queue them. Then make a text from them:
    • Full prompt: everything someone new needs to make the changes, for example for an AI coding assistant.
    • Short prompt: only the changes, for a follow-up in the same conversation.
    • User feedback: plain text grouped into things to fix, adjust, add and questions.
    Copy the text, or save it as a file.
  • Statuses (open → queued → sent → done) change only when you change them. After you really sent a prompt, click Mark these N as Sent. When a change has arrived, set the note to Done.
  • Several notes at once: while notes are ticked, a bar under the list changes their type, area, priority or status, or deletes them.
  • Done list: the Done filter lists finished work; print it or save it as PDF.
  • Undo: Ctrl+Z (outside text fields) brings back the last five deletions.
  • Text size: A− and A+ at the top make the text smaller or larger, the number in between goes back to 100 %; or press Ctrl++, Ctrl+−, Ctrl+0. User notes remembers its size; 100 % is the app's font size.

If you change Peergos Snap's source yourself, enter where it is under Source location for AI prompts (Settings → General) so full prompts can name it. Otherwise leave it empty.

Problems and questions

Nothing happens when I press a hotkey.
Another program probably uses the same keys. Settings → Hotkeys shows a red note next to such a hotkey; choose another combination. The menu always works.
The card says I am not signed in, or that my session has ended.
Sign in again in Settings → Peergos. This happens after you signed out, or after you changed your Peergos password. Until then captures are copied to the clipboard.
The upload failed.
Check your internet connection. Your capture was put on the clipboard and is in the captures folder (menu → Open captures folder), so nothing is lost.
The link was not copied.
Sometimes another program keeps the clipboard busy for a moment. Use menu → Copy last link, or Open link on the card.
How do I capture a menu or a pop-up?
Choose a delay (menu → Delay), start the picture, open the menu during the countdown; the screen then freezes with the menu open. See Capturing a menu.
Where do I find the link of an older capture?
In History…: select the capture and click Copy link.
My Peergos folder is getting full.
Open History…, choose Show: In Peergos, select what you no longer need and click Delete → From Peergos.
My video has no sound.
Videos only get a sound track when your PC played something while recording. Check that Record sound is ticked (menu → Videos). The window after the recording says whether the video has sound.
My friend does not receive my pictures.
You both need Peergos Snap running and signed in, and to be friends in Peergos: open the direct window → Friends… and look at the status under your friend's name. Waiting until they accept means your friend still has to accept the friend request.
A video of a game is black.
Some games in exclusive full screen can't be recorded. Switch the game to windowed or borderless mode.
The mouse pointer is (not) in my video.
Menu → Videos → Show mouse pointer turns it on or off for the next recording.
I can't find the icon.
Click the ^ next to the clock; drag the camera onto the taskbar to keep it visible. If Peergos Snap isn't running, start it from the Start menu.
How do I stop Peergos Snap from starting with Windows?
Settings → General → untick Start Peergos Snap when I sign in to Windows.
Who can see what I upload?
Only people who have the link can open that one file. Your other files stay private. To stop sharing a file, delete it in Peergos.
