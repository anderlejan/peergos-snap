Quick start

Peergos Snap lives in the Windows notification area (the icons next to the clock). It takes pictures and short videos of any part of your screen and gives you a link to share them, or puts the picture or video itself on the clipboard.

  1. Sign in to Peergos. Right-click the Peergos Snap icon → Settings… → Peergos. Enter your username and password and click Sign in. You do this once.
  2. Take a picture. Press Ctrl+Shift+1 (or left-click the icon). Drag a rectangle over the part of the screen you want and let go of the mouse.
  3. Share it. A card at the bottom right says Link copied to the clipboard. Paste the link with Ctrl+V into a chat, an e-mail or a document.
  4. Record a video. Press Ctrl+Shift+2, drag over the area to record and let go: recording starts. Press Ctrl+Shift+2 again (or click Stop), then choose Upload & copy link.
  5. Want the picture itself instead of a link? Right-click the icon → Output → Media to clipboard. Now every capture is copied as a picture or video file, ready to paste.

Press F1 in the Settings window to open the help for the page you are on.

The Peergos Snap icon

The icon is a small green camera in the notification area. If you don't see it, click the ^ arrow next to the clock and drag the camera onto the taskbar so it always stays visible.

Left click

A left click takes a picture, or starts a video, depending on what you chose under Tray click takes in the menu. While a video is recording, a left click stops it.

Taking a picture

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+1, left-click the icon (when it takes pictures), or choose Take picture in the menu.
  2. A short hint appears at the top of the screen. The screen does not freeze: games, 3D worlds and videos keep moving under the mouse, so you can wait for the right moment.
  3. Drag a rectangle over what you want. The size is shown next to it.
  4. Let go of the mouse. The picture is taken at that very moment.
  • A whole window: just click once without dragging. The window under the mouse is outlined before you click.
  • Cancel: press Esc or click the right mouse button.
  • Several monitors: you can drag across monitors.
  • Need a moment after letting go? Set a delay in Settings → Overlay, for example to let an animation reach the right frame.
  • Games: for the best results run the game in a window or borderless window rather than exclusive full screen.

What happens next depends on the output: see Where your capture goes.

Recording a video

  1. Press Ctrl+Shift+2, choose Record video in the menu, or left-click the icon when it is set to take videos.
  2. Drag over the area to record and let go. Recording starts at once (or after the delay you set).
  3. A dashed frame marks the area, and a small bar next to it shows the time with Stop, Pause and Cancel. The frame and the bar never appear in the video, even when you record the whole screen.
  4. To finish, click Stop, press Ctrl+Shift+2 again, or left-click the icon.

Pause and cancel

Pause stops recording until you click Resume; the parts are joined into one video. You can also pause from the menu or with your own hotkey (Settings → Hotkeys). Cancel throws the recording away.

Mouse pointer

Choose whether the mouse pointer appears in videos: menu → Videos → Show mouse pointer, or Settings → Capture. Pictures never include the pointer.

Format and quality

Videos are MP4 by default (plays everywhere); WebM is available too. Frame rate and quality are in Settings → Capture. Videos are recorded without sound.

Where your capture goes

There are two outputs. Switch between them in the menu (Output), in Settings → Output, or with your own hotkey.

Secret link (upload to Peergos)

The capture is uploaded to your Peergos account, into the folder set in Settings → Peergos (normally PeergosSnap). A secret link to just that file is created and copied. It looks like this:

https://peergos.net/secret/z59…/1420289907#egfWi1Wsdjn?open=true

Anyone who has the link can open and view that one file in a web browser, without an account. They cannot change it and cannot see your other files.

Media to clipboard (no upload)

Pictures are copied as a picture: paste them into chat apps, image editors, documents or e-mails. Videos are copied as a file: paste them into a chat, an e-mail or a folder. Nothing is uploaded.

When an upload fails

If you are offline or Peergos cannot be reached, the picture or video is put on the clipboard instead, so you can still paste it (you can switch this off in Settings → Output). The card tells you why the upload failed.

Copies on your PC

  • Every capture is also kept in the captures folder: menu → Open captures folder. Old copies are deleted after 30 days; change that in Settings → Output (0 keeps them forever).
  • To keep every capture in a folder of your own as well, turn on Also save every capture to this folder in Settings → Output.
  • Ask before uploading a picture (Settings → Capture) lets you decide for each picture.

Your Peergos account

Links are created by your Peergos account, so Peergos Snap needs you to sign in once.

  1. Right-click the icon → Settings… → Peergos.
  2. Leave the server as it is (https://peergos.net) unless your account is on another Peergos server.
  3. Enter your username and password. Click the eye button next to the password to see what you typed.
  4. Click Sign in. If your account uses two-factor login, enter the code from your authenticator app when asked.
  • Your password is not kept. It is only used to sign in. Peergos Snap then stays signed in, protected for your Windows account, until you click Sign out.
  • If you change your password in Peergos, you are asked to sign in again.
  • Test connection checks that uploading works.
  • Folder for captures is where uploads go in your Peergos home. It is created when missing.
  • Accounts that only allow a security key (not an authenticator app) as second factor cannot sign in here; add an authenticator app to your Peergos account.

Your shared files in Peergos

Everything you upload is in the capture folder of your Peergos account, where you can open, rename or delete it in Peergos as usual. To stop sharing a file, delete it in Peergos.

Appearance

Choose a colour scheme and the font size for the whole app: Settings, this Help, User notes, the menu, the recording bar, dialogs and notification cards.

  • Dark: Peergos dark, Graphite, Midnight, Forest night, Plum night, High contrast.
  • Medium brightness: Slate, Storm blue, Moss, Mocha, Dusk violet, Teal stone.
  • Bright, soft on the eyes: Peergos light, Paper, Sepia, Mint, Sky, Lavender mist.
  • Follow Windows uses Peergos light or Peergos dark, matching your Windows setting.

Font size goes from 80 % to 160 %; 100 % resets it.

Updates

Peergos Snap looks for a new version when it starts and once a day. When it finds one, it downloads it, checks that the download is complete and genuine, installs it and starts again, usually within a minute. It never does this during a recording or an upload; it waits until you are done.

  • To decide yourself, turn off Install new versions automatically (Settings → General). A card then tells you about a new version, with an Install now button.
  • To look right now, click Check for the new version in Settings → General.
  • After an update a card tells you the new version number. Your settings, sign-in and notes are kept.

User notes

User notes is a list of changes you would like in Peergos Snap: things to fix, adjust or add, and questions. Open it from the menu (User notes…).

  • Add a note with + Add note. Give it a short title, details, a type (fix, adjustment, new feature, question), an area and a priority.
  • Tick notes to queue them. Then make a text from them:
    • Full prompt: everything someone new needs to make the changes, for example for an AI coding assistant.
    • Short prompt: only the changes, for a follow-up in the same conversation.
    • User feedback: plain text grouped into things to fix, adjust, add and questions.
    Copy the text, or save it as a file.
  • Statuses (open → queued → sent → done) change only when you change them. After you really sent a prompt, click Mark these N as Sent. When a change has arrived, set the note to Done.
  • Several notes at once: while notes are ticked, a bar under the list changes their type, area, priority or status, or deletes them.
  • Done list: the Done filter lists finished work; print it or save it as PDF.
  • Undo: Ctrl+Z (outside text fields) brings back the last five deletions.

If you change Peergos Snap's source yourself, enter where it is under Source location for AI prompts (Settings → General) so full prompts can name it. Otherwise leave it empty.

Problems and questions

Nothing happens when I press a hotkey.
Another program probably uses the same keys. Settings → Hotkeys shows a red note next to such a hotkey; choose another combination. The menu always works.
The card says I am not signed in, or that my session has ended.
Sign in again in Settings → Peergos. This happens after you signed out, or after you changed your Peergos password. Until then captures are copied to the clipboard.
The upload failed.
Check your internet connection. Your capture was put on the clipboard and is in the captures folder (menu → Open captures folder), so nothing is lost.
The link was not copied.
Sometimes another program keeps the clipboard busy for a moment. Use menu → Copy last link, or Open link on the card.
A video of a game is black.
Some games in exclusive full screen can't be recorded. Switch the game to windowed or borderless mode.
The mouse pointer is (not) in my video.
Menu → Videos → Show mouse pointer turns it on or off for the next recording.
I can't find the icon.
Click the ^ next to the clock; drag the camera onto the taskbar to keep it visible. If Peergos Snap isn't running, start it from the Start menu.
How do I stop Peergos Snap from starting with Windows?
Settings → General → untick Start Peergos Snap when I sign in to Windows.
Who can see what I upload?
Only people who have the link can open that one file. Your other files stay private. To stop sharing a file, delete it in Peergos.
